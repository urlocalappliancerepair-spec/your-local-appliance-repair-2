Refrigerator Not Cooling? Common Causes and Solutions

Appliance guidance from Your Local Appliance Repair Inc.

Introduction

A refrigerator that suddenly stops cooling can quickly turn into a stressful situation. From spoiled groceries to potential health risks, the impact can be immediate and costly. If you’re searching for refrigerator repair in Brampton, understanding the possible causes and solutions can help you act fast and minimize damage.

Modern refrigerators are complex appliances with multiple components working together. When even one part fails, the entire system can be affected. Let’s explore the most common reasons your refrigerator may not be cooling—and what you should do next.

Why a Non-Cooling Refrigerator Is Urgent

Your refrigerator is designed to keep food at a safe temperature (below 4°C). When it stops cooling:

  • Bacteria can grow rapidly on food

  • Perishable items spoil within hours

  • You may face unnecessary grocery expenses

This is why quick action is critical. If basic troubleshooting doesn’t work, professional help becomes essential.

Common Causes of Refrigerator Cooling Issues

1. Dirty Condenser Coils

Condenser coils release heat from inside the fridge. When they are covered in dust or dirt, they can’t function efficiently.

Symptoms:

  • Fridge feels warm

  • Compressor runs continuously

  • Higher electricity bills

Solution:
Unplug your refrigerator and gently clean the coils using a brush or vacuum.

2. Faulty Thermostat

The thermostat controls the temperature inside your refrigerator. If it malfunctions, the cooling cycle may not start.

Symptoms:

  • Fridge doesn’t turn on

  • Temperature fluctuates

Solution:
Try adjusting the thermostat. If the issue persists, it may need replacement.

3. Blocked Air Vents

Airflow is essential for maintaining even cooling. Blocked vents prevent cold air from circulating.

Symptoms:

  • Uneven cooling

  • Some areas are cold, others warm

Solution:
Rearrange items inside your fridge to ensure vents are not blocked.

4. Damaged Door Seals

A loose or broken door gasket allows warm air to enter, making it difficult for the fridge to stay cool.

Symptoms:

  • Fridge runs constantly

  • Visible gaps in the door seal

Solution:
Inspect and replace damaged seals.

5. Compressor Problems

The compressor is the core of your refrigerator’s cooling system. If it fails, the fridge will stop cooling entirely.

Symptoms:

  • Loud noises

  • Fridge not cooling at all

Solution:
This requires professional repair.

6. Refrigerant Leak

Refrigerant is responsible for absorbing heat. Low levels due to leaks can stop cooling.

Symptoms:

  • Gradual loss of cooling

  • Hissing sound

Quick Troubleshooting Checklist

Before calling a technician, try these steps:

  • Check if the fridge is plugged in

  • Ensure temperature settings are correct

  • Clean condenser coils

  • Inspect door seals

  • Avoid overloading the fridge

If the issue continues, it’s time to seek professional help.

When to Call a Professional

Some problems go beyond basic DIY fixes. You should contact an expert if:

  • The fridge isn’t cooling at all

  • You hear unusual noises

  • There is water leakage

  • The compressor is overheating

Preventive Maintenance Tips

  • Clean coils every 6 months

  • Avoid overloading

  • Keep door seals clean
