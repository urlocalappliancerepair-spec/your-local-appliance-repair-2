Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

Your Local Appliance Repair Inc. respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information of our customers and website visitors.

We collect only the information reasonably necessary to provide appliance repair and installation services, communicate with customers, schedule appointments, process payments, issue invoices, and provide customer support.

Information we may collect includes your name, phone number, email address, service address, appliance information, appointment details, and information you voluntarily provide when contacting us by phone, email, website form, or other communication methods.

We use this information to respond to service requests, schedule and manage appointments, communicate about repairs and installations, prepare estimates and invoices, process payments, provide warranty or follow-up service, and operate and improve our business.

We do not sell or rent customers’ personal information.

Personal information may be shared only when reasonably necessary to provide our services. For example, limited customer information may be provided to an authorized technician or subcontractor assigned to complete a service appointment. These parties are expected to use the information only for the purpose of completing the requested service.

We may also use third-party service providers for functions such as website hosting, advertising, analytics, communications, payment processing, or business administration. These providers may process information only as required to provide their services.

Our website and advertising platforms may use cookies, analytics tools, or similar technologies to understand website traffic, measure advertising performance, and improve our services. This may include services provided by companies such as Google.

We take reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect personal information from unauthorized access, loss, misuse, disclosure, or alteration. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for business, customer service, accounting, legal, warranty, or regulatory purposes.

You may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, subject to any legal or business record-keeping requirements that apply.

Our website may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those websites.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

Website forms and measurement
